# Catwees owner app

Mobile-first clickable HTML mockup. Mode: operate. Audience: a Honda owner in Estonia on a phone, plus a guest who can still finish a task. Sample signed-in person is labeled näidis.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is the work order that follows one Honda. It refuses both the dealer homepage and a card dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: A white sheet on a cool gray desk. Charcoal ink #272D33, secondary #4D5052, stamp red #D62629 only on the stamp, the live 1px edge, and errors. Barlow Condensed for the plate, Source Sans 3 for the form. Ruled lines, rectangular fields, a red-ring stamp. Four states: empty, filled, error, sent.

STORY: A guest reads general notices and can still book. Signing in reprints the same sheet: the stamp takes the owner's name and the plate fills with their Honda. From a notice they book service with a loan car, ask the assistant, or request a test drive or a trade-in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone sheet. Header holds the Honda and Catwees marks and the stamp. The registration number is the largest type. Four folder tabs — Teated, Teenindus, Assistent, Müük — sit under the plate; only the current tab extends into the sheet. Notices are ruled lines with a date and one action. The primary action sits at the foot of a form.

FORM: Workshop job card, fourth on the grounded list, seed a300126e. Signature interaction: login reprints the sheet in one mechanical stamp. Content is already visible. Reduced motion swaps instantly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
